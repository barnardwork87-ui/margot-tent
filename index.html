    Proposal / Tent

    What we're proposing

    Christmas in the bush, Boxing Day on the Wild Coast, New Year on a Karoo wine farm, and a drive long enough that we run out of things to say somewhere near Swellendam and resort to car games.

    The four pillars

    1. Sleep outside.

      Under canvas, mostly. The tents have proper beds. That's the entire difference between glamping and camping, and I'm paying for every inch of it.

    2. Eat like it's Christmas, because it is.

      Braai, mince pies, whatever the lodge is serving. I'll cook at least once. You'll say it's nice. We'll both know. That's teamwork.

    3. Swim in something wild.

      Ocean, river, dam. If it's wet and there's no lifeguard, it counts.

    4. Come back with stories.

      At least three we can tell in public. The rest stay in the Karoo, where they belong.

    Accommodation

    A soft launch at my place in Tamboerskloof. A shower that works, a bed that doesn't fold, and Table Mountain off the deck. You don't even have to lean.
